Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

My Coloring Corner is a coloring app for young children, made by Nicholas Bellinger. We designed it to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. My Coloring Corner does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has:

  • no accounts or sign-ups;
  • no advertising and no advertising identifiers;
  • no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services;
  • no internet connection: the app works entirely offline.

Children's names and pictures

Grown-ups can add a first name or nickname and choose an animal picture for each child, so that each child's artwork is kept separate. These names and the pictures your children make are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can change or delete them at any time in the app's grown-ups area, and deleting the app removes them completely.

Saving to Photos and printing

When a grown-up chooses "Save to Photos" (behind a grown-ups-only check), My Coloring Corner asks for permission to add pictures to your photo library. It cannot see or read your existing photos. Printing uses your device's own print feature and sends the picture only to the printer you choose.

Children's privacy

My Coloring Corner is made for children. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to review, correct or delete. All settings, saving and printing are behind a grown-ups-only check, and the app contains no links out of the app, no purchases and no social features.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
