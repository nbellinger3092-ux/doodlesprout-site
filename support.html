Common questions

Where is the grown-ups area?

On the home screen, tap the small grown-ups button (two people) and answer the multiplication question. There you can change sound, manage children, delete pictures and print coloring pages.

How do I add another child?

In the grown-ups area, under Children, tap + Add a child, type a name and pick an animal. When there are two or more children, the app asks "Who's coloring?" when it opens.

Where are my child's pictures?

Pictures save automatically. Tap the picture-frame button on the home screen to open My Artwork, then tap a picture to keep coloring, save it to Photos or print it.

How do I save or print a picture?

In My Artwork, tap a picture, then the photo button (save to Photos) or the printer button. A grown-ups-only question appears first. Blank coloring pages can be printed from the grown-ups area under Print coloring pages.

How do I delete pictures?

Tap a picture in My Artwork and then the trash can, or delete all of the current child's pictures in the grown-ups area.

Does DoodleSprout need the internet?

No. It works completely offline and collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.
